# The Product Trio

I believe that the Product Operating Model will be the key to success when AI accelerates software development. To enable this mode of working, clear responsibilities and role descriptions are required.

## Product Operating Model in a nutshell

In its essence, the product operating model is about autonomous teams working on ambitious goals. The teams consists of all the necessary skills to make a software product a reality: Design, Engineering and Product Management. These teams usually own a product or part of a larger product end-to-end, and work closely with the end users as well as other key stakeholders.

Some of the questions the product operating model answers are as follows:

- Does the user see the value in our product and buy it? (is the solution **valuable**)
- Does the user know how to use our product? (is the solution **usable**)
- Does our engineering team have the necessary skills and capabilities to create the solution? (is the solution **feasible**)
- Does the product align with our business goals, financial and otherwise? (is the solution **viable**)

The product teams should be able to churn out solutions to business problems in such manner that the answer is yes to all of the above.

### Product Discovery

In the heart of the Product Operating Model is the product discovery work. Product discovery is the ongoing activity of cracking business problems in a way that ensures the above criteria are met. It involves tight cooperation between the Product Trio, as well as the end users of your product.

## Role of Engineering

Engineering mainly **owns the feasibility** aspect of product development. It is our job to ensure we have the necessary skillset to support the technical side of the product strategy set by Product Management, but Engineering also has a say in setting the said strategy.
